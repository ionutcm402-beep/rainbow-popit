Rainbow Pop-It: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Rainbow Pop-It ("the app") is a bubble-popping game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, store or share any personal information, such as your name, email address, location, contacts or photos. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few settings on your device only, such as your chosen colours, sound and vibration choices, and whether Premium is unlocked. This information never leaves your device.

Payments

Premium is an optional yearly subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions. Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app asks for permission to vibrate your device (for the pop feedback) and for internet access (needed to confirm your subscription with Google Play). It does not use your camera, microphone, location or storage.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
